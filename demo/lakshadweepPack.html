Lakshadweep Overview

Lakshadweep, meaning "A Hundred Thousand Islands" , is India's smallest Union Territory and one of the country's most spectacular tropical destinations. Nestled in the Arabian Sea, this enchanting archipelago is made up of 36 coral islands, pristine lagoons, and vibrant coral reefs that offer an unforgettable island experience.

Renowned for its crystal-clear turquoise waters, powdery white-sand beaches, and lush coconut groves, Lakshadweep is a paradise for beach lovers and nature enthusiasts. The islands are home to diverse marine life including colorful coral reefs, sea turtles, dolphins, manta rays, and hundreds of tropical fish species, making it one of India's top destinations for marine tourism.

Popular islands such as Agatti, Bangaram, Kadmat, Kavaratti, and Minicoy each offer unique experiences—from luxurious beach resorts and exciting water sports to rich cultural heritage and peaceful island life. Visitors can enjoy activities like scuba diving, snorkeling, kayaking, paddle boarding, glass-bottom boat rides, deep-sea fishing, and island hopping.

With limited tourism and strict environmental conservation policies, Lakshadweep remains one of India's most untouched destinations. Whether you're planning a honeymoon, family vacation, adventure trip, or simply seeking a relaxing escape surrounded by nature, Lakshadweep promises breathtaking scenery, unforgettable experiences, and warm island hospitality.

4 Days / 3 Nights Itinerary

DAY 01

Day 1 – Arrival & Island Exploration

  • Arrival at Agatti Airport and transfer to your accommodation.
  • Welcome drink and complimentary lunch.
  • Evening tea (available in all packages except Standard Package).
  • Evening sightseeing to Andham Beach, Molokka Beach, Lagoon Beach, Western Jetty Beach, and Eastern Jetty.
  • Enjoy a beautiful sunset at Andham Beach viewpoint.
  • Return to the hotel around 7:30 PM for dinner.

Note: No water sports are scheduled on the first day, allowing guests to relax and enjoy the island's scenic beauty.

DAY 02

Day 2 – Dolphin Watching & Snorkeling

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Dolphin watching cruise.
  • Guided snorkeling session.
  • Complimentary lunch.
  • Leisure time at the beach.
  • Evening tea followed by dinner.
DAY 03

Day 3 – Adventure & Kalpitty Island

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Optional Scuba Diving (Additional Charges).
  • Complimentary Kayaking experience.
  • Lunch.
  • Visit Kalpitty Island with guided sightseeing.
  • Evening tea followed by dinner.
DAY 04

Day 4 – Departure

  • Breakfast.
  • Hotel Check-out.
  • Transfer to Agatti Airport for departure.
