Customized Itinerary & Trip Planning

TRAWELL BROS provides structured itinerary and trip-planning services that bring together the key elements of a journey into a clear, day-by-day travel plan. Their itineraries are designed around the destination, duration of the trip, activities, accommodation, transportation and sightseeing requirements, giving travellers a clear understanding of how their journey will unfold.

What Their Trip Planning Includes

  • Day-by-day itinerary planning with clearly defined activities and destinations.
  • Destination selection and route planning to connect multiple locations efficiently.
  • Sightseeing planning covering major attractions, beaches, heritage sites, cultural landmarks, villages and local experiences.
  • Transportation planning, including airport transfers, inter-city travel, train journeys, private vehicles and ferry/cruise transfers.
  • Accommodation planning according to the destinations and duration of the trip.
  • Activity and experience planning, including trekking, hiking, adventure activities and cultural experiences.
  • Meal and travel inclusion planning, such as breakfast, dinner, refreshments, tickets and permits where applicable.
  • Group coordination and scheduling to keep the trip organized from arrival through departure.

Destination-Based Planning

Their itineraries demonstrate planning around the character of each destination. For example, the Bhutan itinerary connects Thimphu, Punakha, Phobjikha and Paro, with sightseeing and experiences such as Dochula, Punakha Dzong, nature trails, monasteries and the Tiger's Nest hike.

Similarly, their Nepal expedition combines long-distance train travel, border transfer, Pokhara, Ghandruk and Kathmandu, creating a continuous journey rather than treating each destination as a separate tour.

A Structured Travel Experience

The objective is to make the journey organized and easy to follow, with travellers knowing where they will stay, how they will travel, what they will visit and which activities are planned for each day.

In short, TRAWELL BROS's Customized Itinerary & Trip Planning service focuses on converting a travel idea into a structured journey—covering destinations, routes, stays, transportation, sightseeing, activities and key travel arrangements in one coordinated itinerary.

Plan Your Journey With Confidence

By bringing destinations, routes, stays, transportation, sightseeing and activities together in one coordinated plan, TRAWELL BROS helps travellers understand their journey clearly from the first day through departure.
