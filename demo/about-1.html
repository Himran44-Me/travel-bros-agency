About Us

Two Travellers. One Dream. One Journey.

Every travel company has a beginning.

For TRAWELL BROS, it didn’t begin with an office, a website, or a big team.

It began with two people who simply loved to travel — Ajeer and Junaid.

Where It All Started

Before TRAWELL BROS was officially born, Ajeer and Junaid were already helping people travel to Lakshadweep.

In those early days, there was no company behind them. They were simply travellers who knew the destination, understood the local side of Lakshadweep, and wanted to help others experience it.

They helped people with travel plans, local arrangements, guidance, and the small details that can make a big difference when travelling to an island destination.

One traveller became another. One experience became many. And slowly, people began trusting them.

That trust became the foundation of TRAWELL BROS.

Our Roots

Starting from guiding fellow travellers to the hidden gems of Lakshadweep, we learned that true travel is about genuine connection.

The Founders

A

Ajeer

The Traveller Behind the Vision

For Ajeer, travelling was never just about ticking destinations off a list. He wanted to experience places, understand people, discover cultures, and see the world beyond the usual tourist routes.

His journey has taken him across 20+ states in India, as well as Nepal and Bhutan. One of the biggest experiences in his journey was travelling towards Everest Base Camp — an experience that demanded patience, determination, and a willingness to step outside his comfort zone.

  • Every journey taught him something.
  • Every destination gave him a new perspective.
  • And every traveller he met strengthened his belief that travel can change the way we see the world.
J

Junaid

The Other Half of the Journey

While Ajeer was exploring different parts of India and beyond, Junaid was building his own connection with travel and destinations.

His journey through Bhutan added another perspective to their shared passion.

Junaid and Ajeer may have had different travel experiences, but they shared the same belief:

"Travel should feel personal, genuine, and memorable."

That common vision eventually brought their ideas together.

From Helping Travellers to Building a Brand

At first, they never imagined that helping travellers would become a company. They were simply doing what they enjoyed. But as more people started approaching them, recommending them to friends and coming back for their next trip, they realised there was something bigger behind it.

People weren’t only looking for a package
Looking for someone they could trust
Someone who understood the destination
Someone who could guide them
Someone who cared about their journey

That is when the idea of TRAWELL BROS started taking shape.

Why TRAWELL BROS?

The name represents more than a travel business. It represents brotherhood, friendship, adventure, and shared experiences.

We believe the best journeys are not only about the places you visit. They are about the people you meet, the stories you collect, the unexpected moments, and the memories you carry home.

That’s the kind of travel experience we want to create.

From Lakshadweep to the World

What began with Lakshadweep has grown into a bigger dream. Today, TRAWELL BROS is building travel experiences across India and international destinations.

We want to take the same passion, local understanding, and personal approach to every destination we offer.

More Than a Travel Company

We don’t want to be just another company that sells travel packages. We want to be the people travellers can trust before the journey, rely on during the journey, and remember after the journey.

Our own travels taught us that the best trips are not always the ones that go exactly according to plan. Sometimes, the best memories come from the unexpected.

A sunrise after a long journey.

A conversation with a local.

A difficult trek that becomes your favourite memory.

A new friendship made thousands of kilometres from home.

A destination you never thought you would visit.

That’s what travel means to us.

Our Journey Continues

The journey of TRAWELL BROS has only just begun.
