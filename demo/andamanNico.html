Andaman & Nicobar Overview

The Andaman & Nicobar Islands are a breathtaking archipelago in the Bay of Bengal, known for their turquoise waters, powdery beaches, dense rainforests, and a poignant colonial history. Port Blair, the capital, is home to the historic Cellular Jail, a national memorial that tells the story of India's freedom struggle.

A short cruise away lies Havelock Island, officially known as Swaraj Dweep, celebrated for Radhanagar Beach — regularly ranked among Asia's best beaches — along with Elephanta Beach, prime snorkeling spots, and a laid-back island vibe.

Whether you're drawn to history, marine adventure, or simply relaxing on white-sand shores, this Andaman itinerary balances Port Blair's cultural sights with Havelock's natural beauty for a well-rounded island escape.

Destination Features

Historic Cellular Jail and its moving Light & Sound Show in Port Blair.

World-famous Radhanagar Beach and scenic Elephanta Beach on Havelock Island.

Smooth cruise transfers between Port Blair and Havelock Island, ferry tickets included.

Relaxing boat transfer to Elephanta Beach and a leisurely visit to Carbyn's Cove Beach.

Local shopping trip in Port Blair to pick up island souvenirs before you fly home.

Andaman & Nicobar Tour Package Details

Experience the best of Port Blair and Havelock Island with our 3 Nights / 4 Days Andaman itinerary — 2 nights in Port Blair and 1 night on Havelock Island (Swaraj Dweep). This package blends historic sightseeing with pristine beach time for a truly memorable trip.

3 Nights / 4 Days Itinerary

DAY 01

Day 1 – Port Blair

  • Airport pick-up on arrival.
  • Check-in at Hotel Shreesh.
  • Visit the historic Cellular Jail.
  • Attend the Light & Sound Show at Cellular Jail.
  • Overnight stay at Hotel Shreesh.
DAY 02

Day 2 – Havelock Island (Swaraj Dweep)

  • Check-out from the Port Blair hotel.
  • Cruise transfer from Port Blair to Havelock Island (ferry ticket included).
  • Check-in at Radha Krishna Hotel.
  • Visit Elephanta Beach (boat transfer).
  • Visit Radhanagar Beach.
  • Overnight stay at Radha Krishna Hotel.
DAY 03

Day 3 – Port Blair

  • Cruise transfer from Havelock Island back to Port Blair.
  • Check-in at Hotel Shreesh.
  • Visit Carbyn's Cove Beach.
  • Shopping trip in Port Blair.
  • Overnight stay at Hotel Shreesh.
DAY 04

Day 4 – Departure

  • Check-out from the hotel.
  • Airport drop.
