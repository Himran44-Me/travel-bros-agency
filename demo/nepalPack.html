Nepal – Kerala to Kerala Backpacking Trip

Explore Nepal – Group Backpacking Expedition

Beyond Borders • Beyond Ordinary. Travel with strangers, return with stories. A complete Kerala to Kerala backpacking experience through Pokhara, Ghandruk & Kathmandu.

9 Days / 8 Nights

Nepal Trip Overview

Nepal is a land of towering Himalayan peaks, ancient temples and warm-hearted people – the perfect backdrop for a true backpacking adventure. Our Kerala to Kerala group expedition takes you across the India–Nepal border by train and road, from the lakeside charm of Pokhara to the traditional village life of Ghandruk and the spiritual energy of Kathmandu's Thamel Market.

This isn't a regular guided tour – it's a community backpacking experience with the Tripko/Trawell Bros crew, filled with train journeys, village homestays, mountain views, optional adventure sports and free time to explore local markets and street food at your own pace.

Whether you're travelling solo, with friends, or as a couple, this trip is designed to help you disconnect from routine and connect with fellow travellers, Himalayan culture and unforgettable landscapes.

Destination Features

Lakeside Pokhara with stunning views of Phewa Lake and the Annapurna range.

Ghandruk Village trek with a traditional homestay experience in the Himalayas.

Optional adventure sports – bungee jumping, sky swing, paragliding and more.

Kathmandu heritage sightseeing – temples, monuments and traditional landmarks.

Thamel Market experience – shopping, cafes, music and backpacker nightlife.

Nepal Backpacking Package Details

A complete 9 Days / 8 Nights Kerala to Kerala backpacking journey — from train travel and border crossing to village homestays, mountain treks and city exploration. Beyond borders, beyond ordinary.

9 Days / 8 Nights Itinerary

DAY 01

Day 1 – Journey Begins

  • Departure from Palakkad, Kerala at 1:50 PM.
  • Meet the Trawell Bros team & fellow travellers.
  • Start the train journey towards Gorakhpur.
  • Overnight train journey with group games & interactions.
DAY 02

Day 2 – Train Journey

  • Full-day train travel through Indian landscapes.
  • Group games, music & travel interactions.
  • Overnight train journey continues.
DAY 03

Day 3 – India to Nepal Border Crossing

  • Arrival at Gorakhpur (afternoon).
  • Travel towards Sonauli Border & complete formalities.
  • Currency exchange & quick refresh.
  • Overnight road journey towards Pokhara.
DAY 04

Day 4 – Explore Pokhara

  • Arrival at Pokhara, hotel check-in & fresh up.
  • Sightseeing: Lakeside Pokhara, viewpoints, streets & cafes.
  • Relax by Phewa Lake & enjoy the Himalayan atmosphere.
  • Dinner & overnight stay at Pokhara.
DAY 05

Day 5 – Ghandruk Village Trek

  • Breakfast, then travel towards Ghandruk Village.
  • Trek permit procedures & short scenic trek to the village.
  • Traditional Himalayan village life & Annapurna mountain views.
  • Dinner & overnight stay at Ghandruk Homestay.
DAY 06

Day 6 – Adventure & Thrill Day

  • Breakfast, then return journey from Ghandruk.
  • Optional adventure activities: Bungee Jumping, Sky Swing, Sky Cycling, Sky Dining, Paragliding, Boating.
  • Overnight journey towards Kathmandu.

Note: Adventure activities are optional and charged separately.

DAY 07

Day 7 – Kathmandu Heritage Experience

  • Arrival at Kathmandu (morning), fresh up & local sightseeing.
  • Explore cultural heritage spots, temples & monuments.
  • Dinner & overnight stay at Kathmandu.
DAY 08

Day 8 – Thamel Market Experience

  • Breakfast, then explore the famous Thamel Market.
  • Street shopping, handicrafts, cafes & backpacker culture.
  • Evening departure towards Sonauli Border.
  • Proceed to Gorakhpur & board return train towards Kerala.
DAY 09

Day 9 – Return Journey

  • Continue train journey back to Kerala.
  • Share memories, photos & experiences — end of the Nepal expedition.
